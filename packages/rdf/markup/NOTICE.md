The factual named-character-reference data in `entities.ts` is derived from the
WHATWG HTML Standard's normative `entities.json`, retrieved on 2026-10-07:

- Source: https://html.spec.whatwg.org/entities.json
- Standard clause: https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/named-characters.html
- Copyright policy: https://whatwg.org/ipr-policy#copyright

WHATWG and its Steering Group Members own the published document. Section 4.2
of the WHATWG IPR Policy grants implementers permission to copy, modify and
redistribute contributions and materials in original and modified form. This
adaptation retains each name and its `characters` value; redundant `codepoints`
arrays are omitted. The source byte hash is recorded in the TypeScript header.
The table is normative data; no third-party parser implementation is embedded.
